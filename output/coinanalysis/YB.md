## Overview

YieldBasis is an on-chain liquidity protocol solving Impermanent Loss problem in AMM pools. It turns crypto into productive assets starting from Bitcoin. Using Yield Basis, LPs have a direct spot exposure to an asset and earn organic yield with significantly improved risk/reward profile (compared to conventional AMMs).
