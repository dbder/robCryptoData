## Overview

Ambire AdEx (ADX) is a decentralized advertising platform that aims to improve the digital advertising ecosystem by enabling direct interactions between advertisers and publishers while ensuring transparency and efficiency. It utilizes blockchain technology to eliminate intermediaries and reduce costs associated with traditional advertising networks.
