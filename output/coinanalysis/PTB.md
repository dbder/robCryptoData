## Overview

Portal To Bitcoin is a trust-minimized, cross-chain atomic swap protocol that enables fast, low-cost trading across Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other chains without wrapped assets or intermediaries. Portal uses the BitScaler framework to execute hashed-timelock contracts (HTLCs) across chains while keeping users in full control of their keys.
